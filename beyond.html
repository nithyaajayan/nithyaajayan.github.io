Who I Really Am

A Malayali who grew up in Sohar, a small town in Oman. It shaped the way I talk to people, move through places, and make friends with strangers. I like learning about people - where they're from, what drives them, what they're afraid of.

I grew up believing being a "jack of all trades" was a flaw - that you had to eventually pick a lane and stay there. I'm glad I never listened. I am an integration of many people who crossed my path, and I really like to keep it that way. I'm also the first to say yes to any unplanned, mildly questionable mission at 3am.

An Attempting Artist

National 1st in school for poetry, 2020. Guitar since 2019, ukulele too. In second year of college, someone made a racist remark. I wrote a poem about it and stumbled onto something called slam poetry. Performed at my first one a week later and won. I write songs. Someday I want to make things - films, music, something that stays with people. I'm working on it.

The Animals

I have a dangerous affection for animals. I've fostered 50+ stray cats, a dog, and some birds. Nursed them, released them, watched some decide to stay anyway. I want to, someday, build something for them that fosters their needs.

The Mountains

Trekked to Nandi Hills at 3am with the most carefree people I've ever met. Got surrounded by violent dogs in an abandoned street on the way. Survived because I'd already befriended one of them, and an Uber driver who appeared out of nowhere. We made the peak.

Trekked Mount Jibhi at ~10,500 ft with a ligament tear from skateboarding. Explored Manali, Kasol, and Solang Valley. The snow was worth it.

Visited 7 states and 3 countries in a single year — work, wandering, and everything in between.

The Cockpit Tradition

Wanted to check out the cockpit once. Now I talk to pilots after every flight about their lives, their routes, the strange hours. I tell them about mine. I take a photo most times.

Badminton

Picked up a racquet at 4. Played for my school in Sohar, Oman, went onto nationals. U14 Oman National Bronze, 2019. Peak Omani hospitality throughout.

My life's had a good amount of risky decisions and risky choices. But whenever I sit down to regret, I remember that the universe has its way of balancing things out. My vote is always for the things I love, and the ones that hold kindness. I really hope that it stays that way.
